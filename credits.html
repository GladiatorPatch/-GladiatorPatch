NOW WE ARE FREE

Three years of hard work - and the people who made it happen.

3 years of hard work has come to an end today, and I'm sorry if I don't have the right words to say. Three years in which day after day I sat, researched, and worked until the wee hours of the night, until the last minute. All along I didn't give up because I was determined to finish, I didn't give up because it gave me tremendous satisfaction, I didn't give up because the goal was always in front of my eyes - to release the best patch ever and keep what I promised myself.

But all this wouldn't have happened if it weren't for you - the readers, the commenters, the players. Those who were with me when I first started my Evo-Web thread and those who stay with me to this day to advise, help or just give a good review.

This moment is also yours!

And on this occasion, I would like to mention a number of people who helped this moment happen -

Dear @Fabri55, who helped me all the way, who gave me the right tips, who discussed with me day and night about so many topics. And the one that helped this patch create all the missing faces.

The experts @AndrewT and @adams06, who honestly don't know what I would do without them. Their endless help and giving in everything is no small thing, and everything is always with patience and humility.

@Benicio and @jorgito6 the designers, who spent a lot of time to make the best possible patchable design. who had to hear my digs at the smallest details and had to correct again and again, until it was perfect as only they know how to do. The best designers I could ask for.

My friends @Dreamfinder and @Μητρογκόλ, who helped me find the best face for each player and gave of themselves time and effort for the benefit of the project.

The fantastic @FC Bayern, who helped create real kits for all the teams and national teams that were missing, and made sure to make this correction perfect in this aspect as well.

And last but not least, @Sany023 the Wizard, who makes the lives of all of us easier and has been doing it for years with dedication and love.

Thank You

So it's true that I'm not Russell Crowe, and I won't get the Oscar for this patch. But I've been able to really break free over the last three years. To detach from all the diseases of the world, to cling to the true goodness of God and to be a true free man.

My favorite verse in the entire Bible is:

"But the word is very close unto thee, in thy mouth, and in thy heart, that thou mayest do it"
(Deuteronomy 30:14)

Because if you really want something it depends only on you.

Our war against evil is perpetual in this world, but we must fight. Because we have no other choice. Just like a real GLADIATOR.

NOW WE ARE FREE

Yoad Idan
